About

Looking past aggregate metrics

My research centers on analyzing multimodal and language models, and the metrics used to evaluate them, to understand what drives their behavior rather than what their scores suggest.

A failure invisible to standard benchmarks is what I look for: I found that a widely used architectural choice makes retrieval-augmented models largely disregard retrieved evidence, and traced it with ablations and weight analysis.

Before grad school I spent two years as a software engineer building backend and cloud systems, which shapes how I build research pipelines.

  • Trustworthy information extractionDetecting and attributing unsupported claims when models verify each other.
  • Reliable LLMs for healthcareWhat it takes for language models to be dependable in clinical settings.
  • Visual evidence in VLMsHow vision-language models use images when they reason.
